# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in this project is expected to help maintain a respectful, welcoming community. This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews and other project discussions.

Be considerate of different backgrounds and levels of experience. Critique ideas and code, explain your reasoning, and accept corrections. Respect personal boundaries and keep discussions relevant to the project.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

If you experience or witness a violation, contact a repository maintainer through the contact information on their GitHub profile and request a private reporting channel. Avoid posting private details in a public issue. Maintainers will handle reports with discretion and may remove content, limit participation or exclude participants according to the seriousness and persistence of the conduct.

Maintainers are subject to these expectations as well. If a report concerns a maintainer, contact another maintainer where possible. Decisions should be proportionate, explain the conduct at issue and protect the privacy of the people involved.
